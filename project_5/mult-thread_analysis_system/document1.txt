Smart Agriculture and Connected Field Monitoring
Modern farms are adopting IoT sensors to track soil moisture and temperature in real time.
With low-power wireless gateways, farmers can inspect field health from a phone or laptop.
By combining iot telemetry with automated drip irrigation, water waste drops significantly.
Regional cooperatives now deploy IoT weather stations alongside IoT soil probes across large plots.
Battery life remains a major engineering constraint for remote deployments.
When an IOT node loses connectivity, local flash storage buffers readings until the link returns.
Overall, smart farming shows how reliable embedded networks improve crop yield.
