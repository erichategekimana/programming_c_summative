Industrial Automation and Edge Computing
Manufacturing plants use vibration and thermal sensors to predict machine failures early.
Instead of sending raw sensor streams to the cloud, edge gateways filter noisy signals locally.
In an industrial IoT factory floor, millisecond latency is required for safety interlocks.
Maintenance teams schedule part replacements proactively, reducing unplanned downtime.
Standardized protocols such as MQTT and OPC-UA simplify integrating legacy machinery.
Many factories pair IoT controllers with digital twins to simulate production bottlenecks.
Real-time dashboards give plant supervisors a unified view of energy consumption.
Ultimately, iot adoption in manufacturing lowers operating costs and improves product quality.
